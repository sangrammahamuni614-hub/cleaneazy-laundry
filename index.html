GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Have a question we haven’t covered? Get in touch and we’ll help you figure it out.

Which areas in Pune do you serve?+

We’re based in Pune, Maharashtra. Share your neighbourhood when you enquire and we’ll confirm current pickup availability for your address.

How do I know the final price?+

Rates depend on the service and item. We confirm the order details and applicable rates before processing begins. Subscription plans include the weekly allowance shown above.

Can I send delicate or special-care items?+

Yes. We offer dry cleaning for selected garments and separate care for home items, bags and shoes. Let us know what you have when arranging pickup.

How can I follow my order?+

Orders move through clear steps—from pickup and cleaning to quality check, ready and delivery. Customer updates can be sent through WhatsApp once our business line is configured.

How do subscriptions work?+

Each plan has an annual registration amount, monthly payment and weekly weight allowance. We’ll confirm the plan terms and start date with you before signup.
